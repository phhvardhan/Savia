Problem statement - Northwind Pharma

Our warehouse stock levels in SAP S/4HANA do not match the Manhattan WMS. The mismatch is only found during the nightly batch reconciliation, so planners at the Rotterdam distribution centre see wrong availability for up to a day. Customer service promises orders we cannot ship, and Rotterdam reports frequent shipment delays.

What we know:
- The WMS sends stock to SAP once a night as a file over SFTP.
- Any fix must stay GxP compliant (full audit trail of stock changes).
- We cannot change the SAP core; only standard interfaces are allowed.

What we don't know yet: transaction volumes per day, budget, and the target go-live date.

How should we solve this?
