Northwind Pharma - Business Requirements: Real-Time Stock Sync (Rotterdam pilot)
Version 0.3 - approved by Priya Shah, Supply Chain Lead

Background
Planners see wrong stock availability for up to a day because SAP S/4HANA and the Manhattan WMS only reconcile in a nightly batch.

Requirements
REQ-01  Stock movements in the WMS (pick, putaway, adjustment) must update SAP stock within 5 minutes.
REQ-02  Planners must be able to see, per material, the time SAP stock was last updated from the WMS.
REQ-03  If a stock movement fails to post in SAP, the integration support team must be alerted within 15 minutes.
REQ-04  Every stock change made by the integration must be recorded in an audit trail (who/what, before, after, timestamp) to meet GxP.
REQ-05  Integration support must be able to re-post a failed stock movement without asking IT to change data directly.

Users
- Supply planner (Rotterdam)
- Integration support analyst
- QA / GxP auditor

Out of scope for the pilot
- Sites other than Rotterdam
- Changes to the SAP core
